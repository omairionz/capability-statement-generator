SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE  
Department of the Army, Assistant Secretary of the Army for Acquisition, Logistics and Technology (ASA(ALT))  
Tactical Network and Communications Infrastructure Modernization

The Department of the Army, Program Executive Office Command, Control and Communications-Tactical (PEO C3T), is conducting market research to identify qualified small businesses capable of supporting a multi-year initiative to modernize legacy tactical network infrastructure and unify disparate battlefield communications systems onto a resilient, software-defined architecture aligned with the Army's Unified Network vision.

Anticipated scope includes:

- Migration and integration of legacy hardware-defined radio and wireline communications systems into a software-defined, IP-based tactical network fabric
- Implementation of a zero-trust cybersecurity architecture aligned with DoD Zero Trust Strategy requirements and Army Regulation 25-2 (Information Management — Cybersecurity)
- Development of unified network management and situational awareness dashboards supporting command post operations at echelon from battalion through corps
- Establishment of DevSecOps pipelines for the continuous delivery and configuration management of firmware, software-defined radio waveforms, and network policy updates
- Integration with existing Army systems including WIN-T Increment 2 successor architectures, PACE (Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency) planning tools, and ATAK-based mission command applications
- Spectrum management modernization, including automation of electromagnetic spectrum coordination workflows compliant with NTIA and DoD spectrum management policy
- Independent verification and validation of migrated network configurations and interoperability with coalition partner systems under NATO STANAG standards
- Training program development and change management support for Signal Corps soldiers and G6 staff across Active, Reserve, and National Guard components

The Army is particularly interested in small businesses with documented experience supporting DoD or federal defense communications modernization efforts; demonstrated proficiency operating within RMF (Risk Management Framework) authorization boundaries at the Secret collateral level (IL4/IL5 equivalent in cloud environments); experience with MilSatCom, PACE planning, and software-defined radio platforms (e.g., JTRS waveforms, SDR-compliant hardware); and familiarity with Army and Joint Technical Architecture standards including DISR-registered standards and the Army's Unified Data Reference Architecture.

Anticipated contract type: Firm Fixed-Price (FFP) for defined integration milestones, with Cost-Plus-Fixed-Fee (CPFF) provisions for research and development task orders.

Anticipated period of performance: Base year plus four option years.

Anticipated set-aside: Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) sole-source or competitive SDVOSB.

Anticipated NAICS: 517110 (Wired Telecommunications Carriers) with a secondary consideration of 541512 (Computer Systems Design Services) for software integration task orders.

Responses should include: A capability statement with technical approach narrative, past performance examples from comparable DoD communications or network modernization programs (including program office, contract number, and period of performance), relevant certifications (e.g., DoD 8570.01-M/8140 compliant personnel, AWS GovCloud or Azure Government experience at IL4/IL5), and applicable contract vehicles (e.g., GSA OASIS+, Army ITES-SW2, SeaPort-NxG, or GWACs such as SEWP V or CIO-SP4).

All responses are for market research purposes only and do not constitute a solicitation or commitment by the Government. Responses must be submitted via SAM.gov or to the designated Contracting Officer Representative no later than 30 days from the date of this notice. All respondents must be registered in SAM.gov and possess or be eligible for a Facility Security Clearance at the Secret level.
