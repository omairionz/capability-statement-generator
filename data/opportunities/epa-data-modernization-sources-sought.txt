Sources Sought Notice — EPA Office of Mission Support, Data Platform Modernization
The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Office of Mission Support, is conducting market research to identify qualified small businesses capable of supporting a multi-year data platform modernization initiative. The agency seeks to consolidate fragmented environmental monitoring data systems onto a unified cloud-based analytics platform hosted in AWS GovCloud.
Anticipated scope includes:

Migration of legacy on-premise data warehouses to a cloud-native architecture
Implementation of enterprise data governance frameworks aligned with federal data strategy guidance and EPA's data quality standards
Development of advanced analytics and reporting capabilities to support regulatory decision-making
DevSecOps pipeline establishment for continuous integration and delivery of analytics workloads
Independent verification and validation of migrated datasets
Training and change management for EPA program office analysts

The agency is particularly interested in small businesses with documented experience supporting federal civilian environmental, scientific, or regulatory missions; demonstrated AWS GovCloud expertise; and the ability to operate within FISMA Moderate authorization boundaries.
Anticipated contract type: Time and Materials with a fixed-price option for migration milestones. Anticipated period of performance: base year plus four option years. Anticipated set-aside: 8(a) sole-source or competitive 8(a). Anticipated NAICS: 541512 (Computer Systems Design Services).
Responses should include a capability statement, past performance examples from comparable federal data modernization efforts, and applicable contract vehicles.
